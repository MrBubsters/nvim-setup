Neovim Project Playbook

The things you'll actually do inside a project, from opening it to committing your work, and the keys for each. Every shortcut here was checked against the setup installed on your machine.

Space

The one rule: press Space and wait. A menu lists every command group. Below, Space→g→g means press those keys one after another, not together.

Commit and push with lazygit

Spacegg opens lazygit full screen. It has numbered panels: 1 status, 2 files, 3 branches, 4 commits, 5 stash. A typical commit:

  1. Press Spacegg. You land in the files panel, and the right side shows the diff of the selected file.
  2. Move with j/k. Press Space to stage a file, or a to stage everything.
  3. To stage only some lines, press Enter on the file, then Space on each line you want. Esc goes back.
  4. Press c, type the commit message, and press Enter.
  5. Press P (capital) to push. Press q to close lazygit.

Working alongside Claude

Keep nvim and Claude Code side by side in tmux. When Claude edits a file you have open, nvim reloads it automatically once you switch back to it.

Move between tmux panesClicking a pane works too.Ctrlb then arrow
Zoom one pane full screen and backCtrlb then z

Review all of Claude's changes before committingSpacegg

Tip: save before asking Claude to edit a file you're working in. Otherwise nvim will ask which version to keep when you switch back.
